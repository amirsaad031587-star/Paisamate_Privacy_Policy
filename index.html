1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PaisaMate ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal financial details entered in the app.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Financial Records: Transaction amounts, dates, categories, notes, account balances, bills, and budget limits entered to track personal and household finances.
  • Account Credentials: Name and email address if you voluntarily register or sign in using an account.
  • Cash Calculator & Memos: Denomination counts and financial notes saved within the app utility modules.

B. Automatically Processed Information

  • Device Information: Basic device parameters (OS version, device model) utilized strictly for rendering UI layouts, dark/light themes, and biometric hardware verification.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics: Crash logs or anonymous performance indicators to resolve software bugs and improve app stability.

3. Biometric Authentication & Security

PaisaMate offers optional biometric authentication (Fingerprint and Face Unlock) using Android's native BiometricPrompt API to prevent unauthorized access when opening the app.

  • Zero Biometric Data Transfer: Biometric authentication is performed entirely on your device hardware by the Android OS Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) or Secure Element.
  • No Access by the App: PaisaMate never receives, stores, or transmits your actual fingerprint or facial scan data. The operating system only returns a boolean verification status ("Success" or "Failed").

4. Device Permissions

PaisaMate requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver secure and stable functionality:

Permission Type Purpose
android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC Install-time Enables local fingerprint and face unlock authentication through Android BiometricPrompt.
android.permission.INTERNET Install-time Allows optional cloud account authentication, backup sync, and loading test advertisement assets.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-time Checks network connectivity to toggle between online and offline data caching gracefully.

Note: PaisaMate does not request access to your contacts, SMS messages, camera, GPS location, microphone, or external storage files.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

PaisaMate may interface with trusted third-party SDKs strictly to support core infrastructure and development:

  • Google Play Services: Core application utilities and authentication.
  • Google AdMob: Display of test banner/interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect pseudonymous advertising identifiers subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Optional cloud synchronization if user chooses to sign in.

6. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your records at all times:

  • On-Device SQLite Storage: All records are stored locally within the app's sandboxed private storage.
  • Data Export: You can export your full transaction history anytime as formatted CSV, structured JSON, or certified PDF statements.
  • One-Click Reset / Data Wipe: The app includes a "Reset All Data" option in Settings, which permanently purges all local transactions, accounts, notes, and preferences immediately.

7. Children's Privacy

PaisaMate is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.
